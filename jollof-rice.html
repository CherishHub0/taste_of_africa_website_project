Jollof Rice

Nigeria - West Africa

About this dish

Jollof rice is the star of West African parties. Rice is cooked in a tomato and pepper sauce until it turns deep orange. Nigeria, Ghana and Senegal all say theirs is the best, and that friendly competition is part of the culture.

Ingredients

4 servings
  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 large tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper
  • 2 onions
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 0.5 cup vegetable oil
  • 3 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt to taste

Cooking instructions

Step 1: Preparation
  1. Blend the tomatoes, bell peppers, scotch bonnet and one onion until smooth.
  2. Wash the rice until the water runs clear, then drain it.
Step 2: Cooking
  1. Heat the oil and fry the second sliced onion and the tomato paste for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the blended mix and let it simmer for 20 to 25 minutes until thick.
  3. Add thyme, curry powder, bay leaves, salt and the stock, then bring to a boil.
  4. Add the rice, cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat for 30 minutes.
  5. Fluff with a fork and serve with fried plantain or chicken.
