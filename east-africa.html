East Africa

Shared platters, fermented breads and coastal spices.

Food culture

From the Ethiopian highlands to the hills of Rwanda, East African food uses ancient grains, beans, leafy greens and warm spices.

Staples and flavors

  • Teff, maize, sorghum and millet
  • Beans, lentils and greens like cassava leaves
  • Berbere spice, cardamom and cloves
  • Plantains and sweet potatoes

Cooking techniques

  • Fermenting batter to make injera
  • Cooking maize flour into ugali
  • Slow stews called wat
  • Grilling meat over charcoal (nyama choma)
