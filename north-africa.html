North Africa

Fragrant spices, couscous and slow-cooked clay pot dishes.

Food culture

North African food mixes Berber, Arab and Mediterranean influences. Sweet and savory flavors are often combined in the same dish.

Staples and flavors

  • Couscous and flatbreads
  • Olives, dates, almonds and preserved lemons
  • Ras el hanout, cumin, saffron and harissa
  • Lamb, chicken and chickpeas

Cooking techniques

  • Slow cooking in a clay tagine pot
  • Steaming couscous over a stew
  • Preserving lemons in salt
  • Baking bread in shared ovens
