Why we love African food.

Our mission

Our mission is to promote African food heritage by sharing recipes, stories and traditions that deserve a place at every table.

A short history of African cuisine

African cooking comes from thousands of years of farming, herding and trade. Local crops like sorghum, millet, yam and teff fed people long before other ingredients arrived.

Trade routes brought spices across the Sahara and the Indian Ocean. Later, tomatoes, chilies and cassava arrived from the Americas and were quickly made into local dishes.

Today African flavors travel the world with African communities abroad, from jollof in London to injera in Washington, D.C.

A food landmark

Djemaa el-Fna in Marrakech, Morocco fills with food stalls every evening.
