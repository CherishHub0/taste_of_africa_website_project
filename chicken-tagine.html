Chicken Tagine

Morocco - North Africa

About this dish

A tagine is both the dish and the clay pot it is cooked in. Moroccan cooks use saffron, ginger, turmeric and cumin together with salty preserved lemon to make a sauce that is soft, tangy and full of flavor. It is eaten with bread.

Ingredients

4 servings
  • 8 chicken thighs
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 1 preserved lemon
  • 1 cup green olives
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1.5 cups chicken stock
  • 0.5 cup fresh cilantro
  • Salt to taste

Cooking instructions

Step 1: Preparation
  1. Mix the spices with salt and pepper and rub them on the chicken. Leave for 30 minutes.
  2. Slice the onions, crush the garlic and cut the preserved lemon into quarters.
Step 2: Cooking
  1. Brown the chicken in olive oil in a heavy pot, then take it out.
  2. Cook the onions and garlic in the same pot until soft.
  3. Put the chicken back, add the stock and saffron, cover and simmer for 40 minutes.
  4. Add the lemon and olives and cook without the lid for 15 minutes.
  5. Sprinkle with cilantro and serve with bread or couscous.
