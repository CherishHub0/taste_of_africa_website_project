Southern Africa

Braai fires, curries and hearty maize porridge.

Food culture

Southern African cooking mixes local traditions with Dutch, Malay and Indian influences, giving us curries, grilled meat and warm stews.

Staples and flavors

  • Maize meal (pap or sadza)
  • Beef, lamb and dried meat called biltong
  • Curry masala and turmeric
  • Pumpkin and root vegetables

Cooking techniques

  • Cooking over open coals, the braai
  • Slow stews in cast-iron pots (potjie)
  • Drying meat into biltong
  • Baking spiced puddings
