West Africa

Bold, spicy cooking built around rice, yam and stews.

Food culture

West African food is known for big flavor and big gatherings. Meals are usually a starchy staple served with a rich, spicy sauce or soup.

Staples and flavors

  • Rice, yam, cassava and plantain
  • Black-eyed peas and groundnuts (peanuts)
  • Scotch bonnet peppers and palm oil
  • Fermented seasonings like locust beans

Cooking techniques

  • One-pot steam cooking for rice dishes
  • Pounding yam or cassava into fufu
  • Grilling meat over open fire (suya)
  • Deep frying snacks like puff-puff
